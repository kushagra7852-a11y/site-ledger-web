Site Ledger — Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Site Ledger is a site register and contract-management app used by a contractor's team for civil works. This policy explains what the app stores and who can see it.

What is stored

  • Your email address and an encrypted password, for sign-in (Firebase Authentication).
  • The records you enter for a work: daily site entries, measurements, labour and attendance, materials, machinery, bills, letters, expenses and investor entries.
  • Photographs and files you add (site photos, NIT, drawings, approvals).

Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in the Google Firebase project of the organisation that runs this copy of the app (Cloud Firestore). Only people whose email the owner has added to the team list can read or change it. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and a copy is kept on the phone so the app works without network.

What is not done

  • No advertising, no tracking, no sale or sharing of data with third parties.
  • Location is not collected. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo.

Deleting data
